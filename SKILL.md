# Long-runner ad rebuild

A long-running ad is the best free market research there is: brands don't keep paying for ads that lose money. This skill finds those ads, pulls out *why* they work (the structure and the hook), and rebuilds them for the user's brand, with their real product and their own voice. It never copies the competitor's words, images or brand.

## What you need from the user
Ask only for what you can't find yourself:
- Their brand and one product to advertise (name, what it does, price, who buys it)
- 2 to 5 competitor brands, or their category if they don't know competitors
- Their brand words: what they always say, what they never say, any claim rules (health, beauty and supplement brands usually have strict ones)
- Optional: a product photo, their brand colors and font

## Step 1: find the long-runners
Use the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library): country = where they sell, ad category = All ads, search each competitor by name, filter to **Active** ads.
- An ad's "Started running on" date tells you its age. Prefer ads running **60+ days**; 6+ months is gold.
- If several versions of the same ad exist (same hook, different images), that's a winner the brand is iterating on. Note it.
- If you can browse, read the library directly. If you can't, ask the user to paste 3 to 5 ad screenshots or the ad text plus start dates.

Pick the 3 strongest. For each, record: brand, start date (days live), format (static, carousel, video), the hook (first line or headline), and the structure.

## Step 2: name the structure
Match each ad to a structure in `references/ad-structures.md` (for example: objection flip, us vs them, receipt, before/after timeline, "X without the Y", testimonial pull-quote, problem-agitate-solve). Write one sentence on *why* it works for that audience. The structure is what you'll reuse, not the words.

## Step 3: rebuild for the user's brand
For the single best fit, write:
- **Headline:** 3 options, under 40 characters, in the user's voice
- **Primary text:** 2 to 5 short lines. Hook first, product second, the offer or CTA last
- **Description:** under 30 characters (e.g. the guarantee or offer)
- **CTA button:** Shop Now, Learn More or Get Offer
- **Visual brief:** one candid, real-life scene with the real product in it at true size; what's in frame, the light, the props, where the headline sits; no stock-photo look, nothing invented about the product
- **Sizes:** 4:5 (feed), 1:1, 9:16 (stories and reels). Say what moves or crops in each so the product and headline are never cut
- **Why it should work:** one line tying it back to the long-runner ("Brand X has run this structure for 9 months")

Then offer two quick variants: a different hook on the same structure, and the same hook on a second structure.

## Rules
- Never copy the competitor's exact copy, images, logo or brand name. Rebuild the idea, not the ad.
- Follow the user's claim rules exactly. When unsure, use "supports" language and leave it out rather than risk it.
- Write like a person, not a brochure: short lines, specific words, no hype.
- No em dashes.

## Output format
Return a short table of the 3 long-runners you found (brand, days live, structure, hook), then the full rebuilt ad as above, then the two variants. Keep it scannable.
