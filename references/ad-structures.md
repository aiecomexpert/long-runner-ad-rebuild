# Ad structures that keep running

Use these to name what a long-running ad is doing. Reuse the structure, never the words.

| Structure | What it looks like | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Objection flip | Opens with the buyer's doubt ("Not another supplement?") then answers it | Meets skeptics where they are |
| Us vs them | Two columns or a split image: your product vs the usual way | Makes the difference obvious in one glance |
| Receipt | A receipt or price breakdown: the costly way vs yours | Turns value into numbers people trust |
| Timeline / before-after | Day 1, week 2, week 4 | Shows change without hype |
| "X without the Y" | "Matcha without the whisk", "Protein without the chalk" | Names the benefit and removes the pain in one line |
| Pull-quote testimonial | A real review as the headline, product beside it | Social proof does the selling |
| Problem, agitate, solve | Names a pain, makes it vivid, then the product | Classic direct response; works in any category |
| Hyper-specific moment | "2:40pm. The slump has a schedule." | Feels personal; stops the scroll |
| List / reasons | "3 reasons people switch to..." | Easy to skim; each reason is a mini-hook |
| Founder note | A short handwritten-style note from the maker | Human, trusted, stands out among polished ads |
| Unboxing / what's inside | Flat lay of everything in the box | Shows exactly what you get |
| Myth vs fact | "Myth: ... Fact: ..." | Teaches while selling; good for new categories |
